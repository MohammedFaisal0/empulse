# EMPULSE model report

Both models are trained on a **synthetic** historical pool of 6,000 employee snapshots generated by `ml/gen_core.py`. The numbers below show that the pipeline learns the patterns it was given; they are NOT evidence about real employees. Burnout risk is decision support, not a medical or psychological diagnosis.

Method: HistGradientBoosting classifier, Platt-scaled probabilities, 60% train / 15% calibration / 25% held-out test. Explanations are exact Shapley values against the average employee (`empulse_ml.drivers`).
