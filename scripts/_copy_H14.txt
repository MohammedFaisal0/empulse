Rebalancing program began 7 July. Figures aggregated; no group below 5 people.
What happened.
A Q2 delivery surge pushed workload above 115% of baseline in four departments. By July, 61 people were at elevated burnout risk while performance still looked strong.
What we did.
Managers ran 96 workload-rebalancing interventions from 7 July, supported by recognition sprints and targeted training.
What changed.
Elevated risk fell to 38 people. 71% of supported employees improved, and productivity on treated teams rose 3.1%.
What's next.
Platform Reliability and CS · Enterprise need support; extending the playbook to Technology is worth an estimated SAR 0.6–0.8M.
